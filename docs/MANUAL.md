# Player manual

## Playing

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Enter / Space | Start or retry after a defeat |
| Arrows / WASD | Move the fleet |
| P | Pause or resume |
| M | Toggle mute |
| F / F11 | Toggle fullscreen |
| Esc | Leave fullscreen |

On the Web, click the game to give it keyboard focus. Use F for fullscreen
if the browser captures F11.

- **Aim by moving.** Your fleet fires automatically. Read HP above each
  target and its reward below. Destroy alien ships to score.
- **Rescue fighters.** Break a containment ship, then fly into the freed
  fighter to collect the displayed `+N`. A blinking `HELP!` means aliens
  can recapture it. Break the new containment ship to free it again.
- **Grab rapid fire.** Destroy a `FIRE x2` carrier and fly into its `POW`
  module to double your firing rate for ten seconds.
- **Dodge hulls and energy shots.** You lose the run when your last fighter falls.

## Briefing and Top 10

Use Left/Right or the on-screen arrows and dots to browse the briefing
and illustrated lessons. Use the speaker and slider to adjust sound during play.

Beat the tenth score to earn a place in the Top 10. Enter or Space opens
name entry early after a qualifying defeat. Type your name or use the arrow
keys to pick letters, then press Enter to save.
